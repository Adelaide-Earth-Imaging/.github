Adelaide Earth Imaging is a geophysics research group at Adelaide University, developing and applying geophysical methods to investigate the structure, evolution and resources of the Earth.

Our research is centred on magnetotellurics and electromagnetic imaging, complemented by passive seismic, potential-field methods, geophysical inversion and computational geophysics.
